Common questions

Where are my records stored?

Your personal budget is stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it, so make a backup in the app: Menu → Backup and export.

I forgot my PIN code

On the lock screen, tap “Forgot PIN?”. You can confirm the reset with your phone’s own passcode, or get a link by email if you are signed in to an account. The link opens the app with the PIN code turned off.

How do I restore Clams Pro on a new phone?

Open the Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Clams Pro. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

How do I share a budget with my family?

Shared budgets are part of Clams Pro. Tap the budget name at the top left → New budget, then invite people with the QR code. Every member signs in with Google or Apple.
