Delete your Clams account

Clams · Viachaslau Melnik

An account in Clams is needed only for shared budgets. The personal budget has no account: it lives only on your phone and is deleted with the app.

In the app

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The phone needs an internet connection. If the server cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and the app says so.

Without the app

Write to art.melnik.work@gmail.com from any address, with the subject «Clams account deletion», and give the email address you signed in with: of your Google account or Apple ID (if you hid your email with Apple, the hidden address). The account is deleted within ten working days; you will get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Shared budgets you own — for all members, with all their records.
  • Your membership in other people's shared budgets.
  • Your account on the Clams server, your devices and your Pro status.
  • Your Google or Apple sign-in in Firebase Authentication; with Apple, the app also withdraws its access to your Apple ID.
  • Shared budgets on your phone — when you delete in the app. The personal budget stays.

What is kept and for how long

  • Records you added to other people's shared budgets stay there, encrypted, under an account identifier with no email address or name.
  • Server request logs (IP address, time) — up to 30 days after the request.
  • Google removes sign-in data from Firebase backup systems within 180 days.
  • Crash reports, if you allowed them, — up to 90 days; they are not tied to the account.

Details — in the privacy policy.
