SpendLess Privacy Policy

Effective: September 26, 2026

Who is responsible for the app

SpendLess is developed and published by Artsiom Melnik, Republic of Belarus. Questions about your data and requests: art.melnik.work@gmail.com.

This policy covers the SpendLess mobile app for Android and iPhone.

In short

  • Everything you enter is stored only on your phone. The developer does not receive or see this data.
  • The app has no ads, no visit counters and no third-party analytics.
  • The app goes online in two cases: to get currency exchange rates, and for voice input when your phone has no on-device speech recognition for your language. Details below.

The app is not included in the automatic Google or Apple backup. If you change phones or uninstall the app, your data is lost — that is why the app has its own backup (below).

Backups and export

You create backups and table exports (CSV) yourself and decide where to keep them: in a folder on your phone, in your own cloud storage, or sent to yourself. The developer does not receive these files.

A backup can be protected with a password — then it is encrypted. A backup without a password and a CSV file are plain text: keep them in a safe place.

Who receives data

The developer does not share or sell your data: the developer does not receive it. The app itself contacts three outside services:

  • Exchange rates. To convert an amount to another currency, the app requests official rates from the National Bank of the Republic of Belarus (api.nbrb.by, Belarus) and the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu, Germany). The request contains none of your data — only a request for rates. Like any website, the banks see your internet connection address (IP address).
  • Speech recognition. Voice input starts only when you tap the microphone button and only with your permission. The app asks your phone's speech recognition service to work on the phone itself, offline. If your phone cannot do this for your language, the recording is processed on the servers of the phone's recognition service — Google on Android, Apple on iPhone — under their own rules.
  • App store. Installation and updates are handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own rules.

How data is protected

  • Data is kept in the app's private storage, closed to other apps.
  • Access to the app can be locked with a PIN code or fingerprint.
  • Backups can be encrypted with a password.
  • Exchange rate requests use a secure connection (HTTPS).

Your rights and how to use them

Your data is stored on your phone, so you control it yourself:

  • view and change — in the app;
  • export — a backup or a CSV table;
  • delete — delete a record in the app, or uninstall the app: all data is deleted with it.

For questions about your data or requests about your rights (Law of the Republic of Belarus "On Personal Data Protection", Chapter 3), write to art.melnik.work@gmail.com. A request for information about which of your data is processed is answered within five working days; other requests (withdraw consent, stop processing, delete) — within 15 days.

Children

SpendLess is not intended for children and does not collect data about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes — new data, new services, transfer of data to other countries — will be announced in the app in advance, before they take effect.
