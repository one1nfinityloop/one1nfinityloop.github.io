Clamsy Budgets Privacy Policy

Effective: September 30, 2026

Who is responsible for the app

Clamsy is published by Viachaslau Melnik, Republic of Armenia ("we" in this policy). Questions about your data and requests: art.melnik.work@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Clamsy Budgets mobile app (shown as Clamsy on your phone) for Android and iPhone.

In short

  • Your personal budget is stored only on your phone. We do not receive or see it.
  • Shared budgets — the ones you keep together with other people — are synced through the Clamsy server. Their records are encrypted on the phone, and the server has no key: it cannot read amounts, categories or notes.
  • You need an account only for shared budgets: sign-in with Google, or with Apple on iPhone.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics. If you allow it, crash reports go to Google (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • You can delete your account in the app or on the account deletion page.

Backups and export

You create backups and table exports (CSV, Excel) yourself and decide where to keep them: in a folder on your phone, in your own cloud storage, or sent to yourself. We do not receive these files.

The automatic backup, if you turn it on, stays on the phone: on Android in the folder you choose, on iPhone in the app's own folder, which the Files app shows under On My iPhone → Clamsy. If you move a backup from there to a cloud storage, that service keeps it.

A backup is always encrypted with the app's own key, so the file does not open without Clamsy. The app's key is the same in every copy of the app: keep backups where only you can reach them. A backup password set in an earlier version of the app keeps encrypting backups until you remove it, and a backup saved with a password opens with that password. A CSV or Excel file is plain text: keep it in a safe place.

Who receives data

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone beyond the services below.

  • Clamsy server — for shared budgets only. It runs in Google Cloud, region europe-west1 (Belgium). The key of a shared budget is created on the owner's phone and reaches members through a QR code; the server does not have it.
  • Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication, Google LLC, USA) — for shared budgets only, when you sign in. Google processes the email address, the account identifier, and the IP address of the sign-in; Google keeps IP addresses for a few weeks.
  • Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc., USA, through Firebase Authentication) — on iPhone, for shared budgets only, when you choose it. Apple gives the app your Apple ID's identifier and email address — or a hidden relay address, if you choose to hide your email. The app asks Apple for nothing else.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Google) — only if you answered "Send" when the app asked, or turned them on later in Settings.
  • Exchange rates. To convert an amount to another currency, the app requests exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev, an open-source service that collects the rates of central banks; it runs on Cloudflare). If Frankfurter does not answer, the app asks the National Bank of the Republic of Belarus (api.nbrb.by, Belarus) and the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu, Germany); for a currency none of them publishes, the open currency-api data set (cdn.jsdelivr.net or currency-api.pages.dev). A request contains only currency codes and dates — none of your data. Like any website, these services see your IP address.
  • App store. Installation, updates and purchases of Clamsy Pro are handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own rules. Payment details stay with the store; we do not see them. If you sign in for shared budgets, the app sends the Clamsy server the store's proof of your purchase and the start date of your free trial; the server asks Google Play or Apple whether the purchase is valid and until when, and keeps only that.

Transfer of data to other countries

The Clamsy server is in Belgium (European Union). Google and Apple sign-in are processed in the USA, and crash reports are processed in Google data centers, including in the USA. The USA has its own personal data protection rules, and your rights there are protected differently than in Armenia or the European Union.

By signing in with Google or Apple you agree to your email address and account identifier being transferred to the USA. Crash reports are sent only after you answer "Send"; until then the app sends nothing. Your personal budget never leaves the phone.

How data is protected

  • Data on the phone is kept in the app's private storage, closed to other apps.
  • Access to the app can be locked with the phone's own check: Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint, falling back to the phone's passcode. While the lock is on, the app's screen is hidden in the list of recent apps.
  • Shared budget records are encrypted on the phone (AES-256-GCM) before they are sent; the server stores them encrypted and has no key.
  • All connections to the server, sign-in, rates and crash reports use a secure connection (HTTPS).
  • Backups are always encrypted.

Your rights and how to use them

  • View and change — in the app.
  • Export — a backup, a CSV or Excel table.
  • Delete records — in the app; uninstalling the app deletes the personal budget.
  • Delete the account — Settings → Account → Delete account. Shared budgets you own are deleted for all members, from the others you leave; the account, your devices and Pro status are deleted from the server, and the sign-in is deleted from Firebase; with Apple, the app also withdraws its access to your Apple ID. Shared budgets are erased from your phone; the personal budget stays. Without the app — see the account deletion page.
  • Sign out — Settings → Account → Sign out. Shared budgets stay on the phone and stop syncing.
  • Crash reports — turn them off in Settings.

For questions about your data or requests about your rights (Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Protection of Personal Data", Articles 15, 20 and 21), write to art.melnik.work@gmail.com. A request for information about which of your data is processed is answered within five working days. Incomplete, inaccurate or unnecessary data is corrected or deleted within three working days. If you withdraw consent or ask to delete your account, processing stops and the data is deleted within ten working days; we confirm the deletion within three working days after it.

If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Agency of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Armenia or to a court.

After an account is deleted, Google removes the sign-in data from its backup systems within 180 days. The server keeps only the account identifier that the account's earlier records in other people's budgets refer to; it contains no email address or name.

Children

Clamsy is not intended for children under 16 and does not collect data about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes — new data, new services, transfer of data to other countries — will be announced in the app in advance, before they take effect.
